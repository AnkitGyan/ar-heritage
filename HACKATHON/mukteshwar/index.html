Mukteshwar Temple

"The Gem of Odisha" — Bhubaneswar, India

950–975 CE Dedicated to Shiva Kalinga Architecture

Built around 950–975 CE, the Mukteshwar Temple is celebrated as a turning point in Odishan temple architecture. Dedicated to Lord Shiva, this compact yet exquisitely carved shrine introduced the ornate torana — a jewelled arched gateway — which became a hallmark of Kalinga style. Its richly sculpted walls depict scenes from the Panchatantra, celestial figures, and sensuous apsaras, marking a bold new artistic vision that would influence temple-building across the subcontinent for centuries.
